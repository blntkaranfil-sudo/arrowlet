Privacy Policy.

Arrowlet: Arrow Escape Puzzle

Last updated: November 26, 2026
Draft for developer review. Advertising, analytics, billing, age-related safeguards and retention practices must be confirmed against the released Android app before publication.

At BulkarApp, we respect your privacy. This policy describes how information is handled in Arrowlet: Arrow Escape Puzzle (Android package: com.bulkar.arrowlet), how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

Arrowlet is designed to be a relaxing puzzle experience, with progress, daily challenges, cosmetic collections and customizable settings.

Your game, your preferences. Core progress and settings are designed to be stored locally on your device. Connected services, if enabled, have separate data handling practices explained below.

What you should know

01Information we collect

Arrowlet is a casual arrow puzzle game. Information associated with the game falls into the following categories.

Game progress and preferences

The game is designed to save information on your device, including completed puzzles, unlocked worlds, coins, hints, daily challenges and streaks, unlocked cosmetic items, selected themes, language, sound and haptic preferences, and tutorial progress. These records allow you to resume playing and personalize your experience.

Information you provide

If you email us for support or to exercise a privacy right, we receive your email address, the content of your message and any information or attachments you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, payment card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information.

Information from online features

Where advertising, purchases, analytics or diagnostic services are enabled, those services may process device or app identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device and operating system information, language, app interactions, advertising interactions, purchase information, and crash or performance diagnostics, depending on the service and its configuration.

02How information is used
  • To operate the game, remember your preferences and restore your progress.
  • To provide rewards, daily challenges, hints and purchased features.
  • To respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Where enabled, to diagnose errors, improve performance, measure game usage, deliver and measure advertising, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

We use information for purposes related to the game and the services described in this policy.

03Device permissions and local storage

Arrowlet's intended permissions relate to gameplay and connected features:

  • Local app storage: to save progress, rewards, unlocked items and settings.
  • Internet / network access: where needed for connected features, advertising, purchases or support links.
  • Vibration: to provide optional haptic feedback during gameplay. This may be turned off in Settings.
  • Advertising identifiers: may be accessed by advertising services where permitted by your device, applicable law and privacy choices.

Camera, microphone, contacts and precise GPS location are not required for the core puzzle mechanics. Android permissions and privacy settings can be managed from your device. Restricting some permissions or network access may limit connected features.

04Advertising, analytics and third-party services

Online features, when enabled in the version of Arrowlet you use, may be provided by independent service providers. These providers process information according to their own privacy terms and the settings configured for the game.

Advertising and rewarded ads

If advertising is enabled, ad providers may receive identifiers, IP addresses, device information, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and help prevent fraud. Rewarded ads may provide an in-game benefit, such as a hint. Personalized advertising is subject to applicable law and consent requirements.

Analytics and crash reporting

If analytics or crash reporting is enabled, providers may process gameplay events, session information, device information and diagnostic logs to identify technical issues and improve the game.

Google services

Google Play provides app distribution and may process information for its own platform services. Where Google advertising or measurement services are integrated, their processing is also described in Google's privacy resources:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services ↗
  • Google advertising preferences ↗

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or to resolve payment and security issues. Advertising-related disclosures may constitute sharing or targeted advertising under some regional laws.

05In-app purchases

If real-money purchases are available, such as an option to remove ads, purchases are handled through Google Play's billing services. Google processes payment details under its own policies.

Purchase-related information, such as the product identifier, purchase token, transaction status and entitlement, may be used to validate, restore or troubleshoot a purchase. Please do not send full payment card information to us.

Refunds and purchase management are subject to Google Play's applicable rules. You can review your orders and payment settings through your Google Play account.

06Data security and international processing

We aim to limit access to information and use appropriate safeguards for the information we handle. Local game records are managed within the app's storage. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Online service providers may process information in countries other than the one in which you live. Where applicable, such processing must follow the relevant data protection rules and safeguards.

07Children’s privacy

Protecting children's privacy is important. We do not intend to knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable law. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to us may contact our privacy email address.

Where the game is made available to children, advertising, consent and data handling must follow applicable child privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements. Any necessary age-related safeguards must be applied to the applicable features.

If we learn that we have collected children's personal information unlawfully, we will take appropriate steps to delete it, subject to applicable legal obligations.

08Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of targeted advertising or certain disclosures. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority.

Manage your preferences

  • Manage language, sound, vibration and other game preferences in Arrowlet's Settings.
  • Manage advertising identifiers and advertising preferences through your Android or Google settings.
  • Where an in-game privacy or consent control is available, use it to review or update your advertising choices. You may also contact us for assistance.

Submit a request

Email support@bulkarapp.com with the subject “Arrowlet Privacy Request”. We may request limited information to verify your request and will handle it within the period required by applicable law. For GDPR, UK GDPR, applicable U.S. state privacy laws (including California law where applicable), and Türkiye's KVKK, rights and obligations depend on the circumstances and the relevant jurisdiction.

09Data retention and deletion

Local game progress and preferences are kept on the device for as long as needed to maintain your game experience, unless you remove or reset them. On Android you can generally remove locally stored game data through Settings → Apps → Arrowlet → Storage → Clear data. This may permanently erase progress, coins, daily streaks and unlocked items. Uninstalling the game generally removes its app-specific local storage, but device backups or separately held service records may remain.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain relevant records, comply with legal requirements or resolve disputes. Where purchases are enabled, purchase records may be retained for accounting, legal, entitlement and fraud-prevention purposes.

Third-party services retain information according to their applicable policies and configurations. Deleting the app does not automatically delete information separately held by a service provider.

To request deletion of personal information held by BulkarApp, email support@bulkarapp.com with the subject “Arrowlet Data Deletion Request”. We will review the request and explain any legally required or permitted retention. Where appropriate, we will provide guidance for requests that must be made directly to a third-party provider.
